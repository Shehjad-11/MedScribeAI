# Design Direction & Visual Identity (design.md)

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Logo Concept

- **Brand Name Lockup:** **MedScribe** (Bold Slate-800) + **Lite** (Extrabold Royal Blue-600)
- **Symbol / Icon Concept:** A clean, modern medical icon featuring a clinical stethoscope vector smoothly morphing into a spark/check mark, enclosed within a soft rounded circle with subtle blue gradient fill.
- **Brand Personality:** Trustworthy, professional, clinical-grade, fast, clean, and empowering for rural healthcare providers.

---

## 2. Color Palette Rationale

- **Primary Brand Color (Royal Blue `#2563EB` / `blue-600`):** Communicates clinical precision, reliability, and modern medical technology.
- **Secondary Surface Neutral (Slate `#0F172A` to `#F8FAFC`):** Slate grey provides high contrast for clinical readability without harsh pure-black eye strain during long clinical shifts.
- **Clinical Functional Accents:**
  - **Emerald Green (`#059669`):** Indicates objective clinical findings, verified SOAP notes, and successful actions.
  - **Indigo (`#4F46E5`):** Highlights clinical assessments, working impressions, and diagnostic coding.
  - **Amber / Orange (`#D97706` / `#EA580C`):** Highlights drug interaction alerts, missing information warnings, and clinical uncertainty flags requiring physician verification.
  - **Rose / Red (`#DC2626`):** Reserved strictly for active recording indicators, severe contraindications, and critical error banners.

---

## 3. Typography Choice

- **Primary Font Family:** Inter / System Sans-Serif (`font-sans` with `font-mono` fallback for clinical vitals, lab numbers, and ICD-10/CPT codes).
- **Scale:**
  - **Header Titles:** 18px (`text-lg font-bold tracking-tight`)
  - **Section Headings:** 14px (`text-sm font-bold uppercase tracking-wider`)
  - **Body Content:** 13px - 14px (`text-xs sm:text-sm leading-relaxed`)
  - **Badges & Labels:** 10px - 11px (`text-[10px] font-bold uppercase`)

---

## 4. Landing Page Wireframe (Plain-Language Description)

1. **Header Navigation:**
   - Left: MedScribe Lite logo lockup.
   - Center: Quick links to Product, Problem, Workflow, Pricing.
   - Right: "Launch Workstation" primary button.

2. **Hero Section:**
   - Headline: "AI Clinical Documentation Built for Low-Resource & Rural Primary Care."
   - Subheadline: "Transform ambient consultation voice dictations into verified SOAP notes, ICD-10/CPT coding, and drug safety alerts in under 2 minutes."
   - CTA Buttons: "Try Sample Scenarios" (Primary) and "View Interactive Demo" (Secondary).

3. **Problem & Impact Section:**
   - 3-card grid contrasting the old way (40% consultation time spent typing, delayed care, missed billing codes) vs. the MedScribe Lite way (12+ minutes saved per encounter, automatic safety audits, physician review gate).

4. **Product Screenshot / Interactive Preview Section:**
   - High-fidelity mockup showing the 4-quadrant Bento layout: Patient Demographics Form, Consultation Dictation Workspace, Structured SOAP View, and Billing/Safety Panels.

5. **Pricing Tiers Section:**
   - **Community Outreach Tier (Free / Open Source):** For single rural clinics, offline local encounter storage, standard SOAP generation.
   - **Health Center Tier ($19/mo):** For primary care centers, multi-user sync, FHIR JSON export, priority processing.
   - **District Health Network (Custom):** For regional health ministries, custom EHR integrations, localized drug interaction databases.

6. **Call-to-Action (CTA) Footer:**
   - Bold banner: "Ready to eliminate clinical documentation burden in your clinic?" with a direct link button to launch the workstation app.
