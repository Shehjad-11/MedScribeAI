# Project Context & Overview (project-context.md)

---

## 1. Problem Statement

In low-resource primary care settings—such as rural district health centers, community clinics, and mobile outreach units across developing regions and underserved areas—healthcare workers face overwhelming patient volumes (often 40+ consultations per day per clinician). Administrative burden consumes up to 40% of consultation time. Clinicians spend valuable minutes manually writing clinical SOAP notes, looking up ICD-10/CPT billing codes, and checking for dangerous drug-drug or drug-disease interactions. This leads to clinician burnout, compromised patient documentation, missing safety audits, and revenue loss for primary care centers.

---

## 2. Target Users & Target Segment

- **Primary Target Segment — Small Independent Clinics & Community Health Centers (CHCs):** Low-resource outpatient facilities, rural health posts, and small practice teams requiring fast, reliable clinical documentation and automated safety auditing without costly enterprise EHR infrastructure.
- **Community Health Centers (CHCs) & Primary Care Units:** Outpatient clinicians handling high daily patient volumes, acute visits, chronic disease management (hypertension/diabetes), and maternal-child health checkups.
- **Solo & Small-Group Outpatient Clinicians:** Independent rural primary care providers needing instant dictation-to-SOAP conversion, drug interaction safety guardrails, and printable patient prescription slips.

---

## 3. Current Project Stage

- **Status:** Post-hackathon proof-of-concept transitioning into a production-ready, startup-grade clinical software product.
- **Current Capability:** Fully functional React + Express stack connected to Gemini 3.6 Flash for structured SOAP generation, safety interaction alerts, billing coding recommendations, offline local encounter history, and printable prescription slips.
- **Transition Goals:** Establishing rigorous technical governance, brand identity polish, modular code refactoring, test harness setup, and open-source compliance posture.

---

## 4. Elevator Pitch

> **MedScribe Lite** is an AI-powered clinical documentation and safety assistant specifically tailored for low-resource and rural primary care clinics. By turning unstructured doctor-patient consultation transcripts or ambient voice dictation into verified, structured SOAP notes, ICD-10/CPT billing codes, and instant safety alerts in seconds, MedScribe Lite reduces documentation time from 15 minutes down to under 2 minutes per patient—enabling rural healthcare workers to focus on saving lives.
